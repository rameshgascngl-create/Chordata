# Wave 6 — original Scoliodon-type brain dorsal anatomy

Diagram ID: u2-scoliodon-brain-labelled

Original vector illustration, rendered to offline PNG. It is not a copied textbook image or a genuine Rhizoprionodon acutus brain dissection.

Dorsal anatomy: olfactory sacs separated from bilobed bulbs, elongated tracts, smooth cerebrum, short diencephalon/pineal stalk, partly obscured optic lobes, folded cerebellum, bilateral corpora restiformia, tapering medulla and spinal cord. No ventral-only glands or optic chiasma are misrepresented as dorsal organs.
