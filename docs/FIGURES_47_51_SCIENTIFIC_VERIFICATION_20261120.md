## Figure 47 — Male and female *Columba livia* urinogenital anatomy
- Original PNG SHA-256 `f0678127364b73f72dff3a857aa61a0fae56715003c1805d86606ad679630404` retained; figure mapping, displayed labels, owner provisional integration and AI provenance preserved.
- Two male testes, separate vasa deferentia, two kidneys, paired ureters and the cloaca are depicted. The female panel depicts a single prominent ovary, a continuous oviduct and the same urinary pathway.
- **Scientific teaching-key correction:** the labelled avian **uterus (shell gland)** is one portion of the left oviduct where shell formation occurs. It is **not the uterus of a placental mammal**, and it is not a placenta. The correct sequence is infundibulum → magnum → isthmus → shell-gland/uterus → vagina → cloaca.
- **Tamil terminology corrected:** the incorrect `பொதுக்கழிவறை` was replaced with `பொதுக்கழிவு அறை`; female reproductive terms use `இடது சினைப்பை` and `இடது முட்டைக்குழாய்`.
- Remains unresolved: the AI plate lacks an anatomical orientation marker and independently validated organ scale; the precise spatial relationship of the ducts requires comparison with an authorised original dissection. A readable label does not itself validate an AI-generated organ topology.
- Reference plates and text: Parker's *Columba livia* male/female drawings, https://etc.usf.edu/clipart/48600/48604/48604_male_pigeon.htm and https://etc.usf.edu/clipart/48600/48605/48605_female_pigeo.htm ; eGyanKosh Comparative Chordates Unit 9 https://www.egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/16528/1/Unit-9.pdf .

## Figure 51 — Avian respiration (L. Shyamal)
- Canonical English PNG SHA-256 `c3d43b9dbdf71d41b173d2543f4c51495b699ed2d96c3d2667370672c8bbacb5` retained; the separately registered Tamil alternative is unchanged.
- The two-panel original drawing depicts inhalation and exhalation. Small arrows **outside** the sac outlines represent expansion during inspiration and contraction during expiration; they are **not arrows for bulk airflow through the sacs**.
- New English/Tamil key explicitly distinguishes posterior-sacs-to-parabronchi ventilation, anterior-sac outflow and gas exchange within the lung, and explains that this two-phase plate is not the complete path of an individual air parcel across multiple breaths.
- Remaining verification: the source's simplified parabronchial paths should be read against the original legend; the Tamil AI alternative is not a reliable map of individual air sac counts or bronchial topology. Do not promote either variant to final independent approval.
- Source: L. Shyamal, Wikimedia Commons https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BirdRespiration.svg ; experimental ventilation physiology https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3763801/ and modern review https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11864839/ .
