## Corrections committed

1. **Marsupial gestation and placenta (`u5-marsupials/short-gestation`):** The earlier wording could imply that only a small minority of marsupials have a maternal–foetal exchange surface. English and Tamil now explicitly describe the common **choriovitelline/yolk-sac placenta**, and the additional **chorioallantoic placenta** observed in certain lineages including bandicoots. This is not a claim that their placentas are identical to eutherian placentas.
2. **Bat echolocation (`u5-flying-mammals/echolocation`):** Removed reliance on obsolete 'microchiropteran' terminology to characterise sonar. Both languages now distinguish predominant laryngeal sonar from **lingual click echolocation in Egyptian fruit bats, *Rousettus aegyptiacus***. A fruit diet is not a reliable yes/no predictor of echolocation.
3. **Bat physiology (`u5-flying-mammals/flight-physiology`):** Added a missing Tamil explanation of short daily torpor versus prolonged hibernation and the important physiological constraints during pregnancy and lactation. The original English and Tamil points are retained.
