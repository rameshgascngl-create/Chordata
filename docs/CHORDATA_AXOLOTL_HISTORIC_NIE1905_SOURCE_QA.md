Biology warning: historical Fig. 1 gilled aquatic, Fig. 2 metamorphosed terrestrial-looking form. The older source's 'adult' label describes the metamorphosed form, not the common adult axolotl; this species usually matures while retaining external gills and tail fin. Metamorphosis is rare or experimentally induced. Compare the forms, never teach obligatory transformation.
