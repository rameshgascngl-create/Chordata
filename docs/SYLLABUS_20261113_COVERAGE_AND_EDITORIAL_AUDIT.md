## Scientific corrections that must remain visible to learners

1. *Balanoglossus* has a stomochord rather than a true chordate notochord; Hemichordata is now a distinct phylum.
2. Traditional "Pisces", "Labyrinthodontia", "Cotylosauria", "Parapsida", and bird-excluding "Reptilia" are historic syllabus categories, not unqualified modern monophyletic units.
3. The photographed syllabus uses *Scoliodon sorrakowah*; fisheries taxonomies commonly accept *S. laticaudus*. Reference: https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?id=217364&p=taxdetails
4. The photographed syllabus uses *Rana hexadactyla*. Current taxon treatments differ: ITIS, referencing Amphibian Species of the World, recognises *Phrynoderma hexadactylum*, while other catalogues retain *Euphlyctis hexadactylus*. Reference: https://itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?search_topic=TSN&search_value=1270588
5. *Calotes* has a three-cavity functionally divided ventricle, not an invented complete mammalian four-chambered heart.
6. Bird respiratory air sacs ventilate the parabronchial lungs; they are not mammalian alveoli. Rabbit has lagomorph incisors, caecotrophy and a duplex uterus.
7. Medical snake identification cannot rely on head shape/pupil alone, and bite severity cannot be judged from fang marks.
