## Additional urgent figure outside the 23: Scoliodon brain
- Registered ID: `u2-scoliodon-brain-labelled`, Unit II, `u2-scoliodon`, page 4, section `brain`.
- Current `pencil_plates/shark_brain_dorsal_en.png`: 1420 × 1010 px, SHA-256 `8d61c1196f020158f865ee0434c66ac70323e3842d874d47dcf23a812f5da9ef`. This combines a **500×941** historical 1878 dogfish-brain engraving (Gustav T. Fritsch) with a text panel, and does **not establish `Scoliodon laticaudus` specimen identity**. Visual review register says user rejected existing anatomical illustration quality.
- Phone-scale problem: at image-fit width of 360 px, the embedded original illustration is at most ~127 px wide (360 × 500 / 1420), before UI paddings, unless the student zooms. Fine original abbreviations are therefore poor in an unzoomed lesson view.
- A new original AI 1024×1536 PNG was generated, SHA-256 `b43141980fe6788a8e0512da305dc9da3b2f525627a15415929f817bb4d37ace`, but **REJECTED** as a teaching replacement: cerebrum appears as strongly paired mammalian-like hemispheres and cerebellum is overfolded. AI was then inconsistent and generated unrelated pigeon graphics. Those are discarded; nothing of these candidates is installed.
- Direct copying from `Fish Biology` (Bhumi Publishing, 2022) is not permitted without authorisation; it expressly reserves reproduction rights. Hansraj College teaching slides are **not licence-cleared** either. Use as topology reference only, with citation.
- Required dorsal-view topology (subject to expert assessment): olfactory sacs, bilobed lobes, paired peduncles, enlarged smooth cerebral region, pineal stalk, optic lobes partly exposed beside cerebellum, folded cerebellum, paired restiform bodies, medulla, spinal cord. Do not introduce ventral-only pituitary, optic chiasma, or saccus vasculosus into a dorsal plate.
- **Taxonomic pitfall:** historic `Scoliodon terraenovae` is presently a synonym of `Rhizoprionodon terraenovae`, not the Indian `Scoliodon laticaudus`: https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?id=158510&p=taxdetails
